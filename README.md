## About Me

Systems Analysis and Development student focused on **software development, automation, systems integration, AI architecture, and infrastructure**.

I enjoy building useful tools and understanding how systems work under the hood: from APIs and databases to Linux, cloud infrastructure, and AI systems.

Right now I'm building open-source tools for AI agents: how they choose a model, how they act in the browser and on the desktop, and how a person stays in control of what they do.
